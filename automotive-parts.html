Automotive Parts & Hardware

Precision injection molds for automotive and hardware plastic parts, with tolerances agreed per drawing and repeatable quality.

What we can make for this industry

  • Functional and structural plastic parts
  • Precision dimensions with controlled tolerances
  • Durable tooling for high-volume production
  • Provide your required quality and PPAP document list for review; support is confirmed per project.

Typical materials we mold

  • PP / ABS / PA6 / PA66
  • Glass-filled and reinforced grades
  • POM for precision mechanical parts
  • High-heat and impact-modified materials as specified

Tell us your material and tolerance requirements — every part is quoted and made per your drawing and specification.

After our first conversation

Once we start chatting, we can review drawings and agree the material, inspection and delivery requirements. NDA available before sharing files.

  • Drawing revision, mating features, critical dimensions and tolerances.
  • Specify the resin grade, reinforcement, color and required operating conditions.
  • List inspection criteria and any required customer quality or PPAP documents for confirmation.
  • Provide trial, batch and expected repeat-order quantities, plus the requested delivery date.
