Home Appliances

Reliable injection molds and molded parts for home appliances, built for consistent repeat production by a Dongguan mold factory.

What we can make for this industry

  • Appliance housings and covers
  • Internal frames and functional parts
  • Consistent color and texture across batches
  • Steel and tooling designed for long production runs

Typical materials we mold

  • ABS / HIPS / PP
  • PC and flame-retardant grades
  • PA / POM for mechanical parts
  • Heat- and impact-resistant compounds as required

After our first conversation

Once we start chatting, we can review drawings and agree how the housing or functional part will be used and checked. NDA available before sharing files.

  • Overall dimensions, wall thickness and critical fit requirements.
  • Specify resin grade, color, texture and the appearance standard for visible surfaces.
  • List heat, impact, flame or other required tests, including any food-contact requirements.
  • Provide initial and repeat-batch quantities, sample approval needs and the requested delivery date.
