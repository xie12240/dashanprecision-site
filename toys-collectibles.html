Toys & Collectibles

Injection molds for toys, figures and collectibles, with the fine detail and part quality those products demand.

What we can make for this industry

  • Action figures, parts and accessories
  • Fine surface detail and textures
  • Multi-piece assemblies with consistent fit
  • Short-run to mass production flexibility

Typical materials we mold

  • ABS / PP / PE
  • GPPS / HIPS
  • TPE / soft parts
  • Specify required material grades and safety testing; suitability and documents are confirmed per project.

After our first conversation

Once we start chatting, we can review the design and acceptance requirements for the figure, accessory or assembled part. NDA available before sharing files.

  • Fine details, textures and parting-line preferences in drawings or a reference sample.
