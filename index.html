How we work

One team, from the first chat to production.

Start with the part you need. We work through the details together.

  1. Talk about your part

    Tell us the application and quantity. After we connect, share drawings with an NDA if needed.

  2. Review & quote

    We review material, fit and tooling needs, then confirm scope and a quote.

  3. Make & trial the mold

    Our team machines and assembles the mold, then reviews trial parts with you.

  4. Produce your parts

    Once you approve the trial, we agree production, inspection and delivery details.
