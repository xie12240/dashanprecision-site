About Us

Who We Are

Founded in 2021 in Hengli, Dongguan — the heart of China's mold industry — DASHAN Precision is a one-stop partner for precision molds and injection molding. Located in the most complete mold-industry cluster in China, we tap into a mature supply chain to make every mold faster, more reliable and more cost-effective.

We make molds and parts per your drawings — from single prototype molds to production tooling and molded parts, with stable quality and on-time delivery.
