FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

Straight answers about drawings, molds, lead times and confidentiality. Anything else? Ask us directly.

What file formats do you accept for drawings?

We accept STEP / STP, IGES, STL, PDF, DWG and DXF. You can upload a file through the request-a-quote form on our contact page, or send it directly via WhatsApp or email.

Can you make molds from my drawing / design?

Yes. We are an OEM / ODM manufacturer and work from your drawings. We also give a free DFM (design for manufacturability) review and flag anything that could save cost or improve quality before we cut steel.

Do you make both the mold and the parts?

Yes. We are a one-stop partner: precision mold making, injection molding and CNC-machined parts. You can order a mold only, molding only, or full turnkey (mold + mass production).

What is your typical lead time?

As a reference, our quotation states production in about 30-45 days after deposit; samples usually take about 5-10 days. Exact timing depends on mold complexity and is confirmed with your order.

What is your MOQ?

MOQ depends on the product and is stated per quotation. Mixed-container shipments are welcome, and for genuine first orders we can discuss a smaller trial quantity. Tell us your project and we will be specific.

How do you keep my drawings and design confidential?

Your data is treated as confidential. We can sign an NDA before we receive drawings if your company requires it.

What are your payment and trade terms?

As a reference, our quotation uses EXW Dongguan with 100% T/T in advance; freight and customs are arranged by the buyer. Other terms can be discussed for repeat or larger orders.

What plastic materials can you mold?

We mold a broad range of thermoplastics and engineering materials. Send us your part requirements (material, color, application) and we will confirm suitability and recommend options.

Can I visit or inspect your factory?

Yes. Our real factory and workshop are in Hengli Town, Dongguan, China. We also provide real workshop photos and videos before you commit, and can arrange samples or third-party inspection.

Do you only make molds for one industry?

No. We serve consumer electronics, home appliances, automotive parts & hardware, toys & collectibles and medical devices, plus custom projects of all kinds.
