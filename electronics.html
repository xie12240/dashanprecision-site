Industries

Consumer Electronics

Injection molds and molded parts for consumer electronics: housings, frames, buttons and structural parts made to your drawings in Dongguan, China.

What we can make for this industry

  • Precision housings and enclosures for electronics
  • Structural frames, brackets and internal parts
  • Buttons, keys and small functional components
  • Draft angles, thin-wall and tight-tolerance features per drawing

Typical materials we mold

  • ABS / PC / PC-ABS
  • Nylon (PA) and glass-filled grades
  • TPU / TPE for soft-touch and seals
  • Custom colors and surface textures (MT / SPI finishes)

Tell us your material and tolerance requirements — every part is quoted and made per your drawing and specification.

After our first conversation

Once we start chatting, we can review drawings and agree the enclosure, surface and assembly requirements. NDA available before sharing files.

  • Enclosure dimensions, wall thickness and critical assembly tolerances.
  • Identify visible surfaces, button travel or fit, texture and color requirements.
  • Specify the resin grade and any required heat, flame or electrical tests for review.
  • Provide sample and production quantities, mating-part information and the requested delivery date.
